- BY DESIGN: iss is the CampusHub AuthServer while aud and the custom claims are campus-store's.
  That pairing is not a mistake and must not be "made consistent". It is how OIDC really works:
  ONE identity provider issues tokens for MANY applications, and custom claims are namespaced by
  the application that defines them -- so a token issued by our provider FOR the campus-store app
  correctly carries campus-store-namespaced claims. Seed/Clients.cs:43 configures the LESSON DEMO
  client only; it is not a claim that the AuthServer can emit just one namespace.

  Day-12 primer and Day 16, campushub-api/roles in Lab 2). Seeing more than one is the point: the
  namespace is arbitrary, and the transferable skill is making RoleClaimType name whatever YOUR
  provider actually emits. Unifying them is a separate parked decision -- see the same note in
  Day-16/Student-Reference/esl-changelog.md. Adjudicated Session 689.
